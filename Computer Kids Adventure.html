Computer Storage: Cloud vs. Local

Where do your photos and school homework sleep?

☁️

Cloud Storage

Internet
  • 🌐 Where: Stored on the INTERNET
  • 🌍 Access: Access from ANYWHERE around the world
  • 📶 Requires: Needs WiFi / Data to open & save
  • 🛡️ Safety: SAFE even if your tablet breaks!
  • 📂 Examples: Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, iCloud
💾

Local Storage

Device
  • 📱 Where: Stored inside YOUR DEVICE
  • ⚡ Speed: Super fast, no waiting for download!
  • 📴 Offline: Works perfectly WITHOUT internet
  • ⚠️ Risk: Files can be lost if device breaks
  • 📂 Examples: USB Flash Drive, Hard Disk Drive, Memory Card

Using the Internet & Safety Rules 🛡️

The Internet is a huge network connecting computers around the world!

📶 3 Things Needed to Access Internet:

  1. Computer Device: Phone, Laptop, iPad, Tablet
  2. Data Connection: WiFi or Mobile Data
  3. Web Browser: Chrome, Safari, Firefox, MS Edge

🔍 Trusted Website Endings:

.ac
Academic / School
.gov
Government
.org
Non-profit

🛡️ 5 Golden Safety Tips for Kids:

1️⃣ Do NOT share personal information (address, full name).
2️⃣ Use a strong password (mix letters & numbers).
3️⃣ Do NOT talk to strangers online!
4️⃣ Do NOT click on strange links or popup ads.
5️⃣ Tell an adult if something makes you feel uncomfortable.
