🌻 Little Gardener Lessons

How Do Plants Get Their
Amazing Traits?

Watch these 5 tiny 3D gardens to learn how farmers and scientists help plants grow the way we want!

1

True Breeding

Parents make babies that look just like them!

When a plant is "true breeding," it means if you let it make babies with another plant that is exactly the same, ALL the babies will look the same too — every single time!

EXAMPLE A tall pea plant 🌱 + a tall pea plant 🌱 = tall baby plants, every time!
2

Seed Breeding

Pick the best seeds, grow the best plants!

Farmers look at their plants and pick the seeds from the strongest, healthiest ones. Then they plant those seeds next season to grow even better plants!

EXAMPLE A farmer picks rice seeds 🌾 from the strongest plants with the most grains, and plants those next year.
3

Cross Breeding

Mixing two different plants to get the best of both!

Scientists take two different kinds of plants — each one good at something different — and breed them together. They hope the babies will have both good traits!

EXAMPLE Rice A 🌾 (great in dry weather) + Rice B 🌾 (grows lots of food) = new rice that hopefully does both!
4

Cross Pollination

A busy bee carries pollen between flowers!

Pollen is like tiny yellow dust that flowers need to make seeds. When a bee flies from one flower to a totally different flower and drops pollen there, that's cross pollination!

EXAMPLE A bee 🐝 visits a sunflower on Plant A, then flies to Plant B and leaves pollen there.
5

Pollination Makes Seeds

Pollen + flower = brand new seeds!

Once pollen lands on a flower, something magical happens — the flower can now grow a seed inside it! That tiny seed can grow into a whole new plant one day.

EXAMPLE Pollen lands on a flower 🌼 → the flower grows a little seed pod → a new seed is born!
💡 Note for teachers: "seed pollination" isn't an official science term — it simply describes pollination leading to seed formation.
